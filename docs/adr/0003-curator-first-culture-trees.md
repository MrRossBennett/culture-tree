# Prioritize curator-authored Culture Trees

Culture Tree will prioritize curator-authored Culture Trees made from Recognized Cultural Subjects, with AI as assistance for starting, growing, and discovering rather than the core identity of the product. A Culture Tree is held together by Curatorial Intent, so explicit Branch-to-Branch explanations, Guide Sections, and AI-generated structure are optional rather than required.

## Considered Options

- Lead with AI generation as the primary product loop: enter a Seed, generate a tree, then edit or share it.
- Lead with broad capture like a visual bookmarking product, allowing arbitrary links, uploads, and media.
- Lead with curator-authored Culture Trees of recognized cultural subjects, using AI to help when useful.

## Consequences

Manual curation, Add to Tree, Branch Type filters, Liked Branches, Follow Curator, and Public Trees should become first-class product surfaces. Raw links and uploads should not become Branches in the current product model; they may become supporting references later only if they resolve to cultural subjects. Free-plan messaging should center the Tree Creation Limit, while AI Generation Allowance remains the cost-control mechanism for AI-assisted actions.
